## About Appinion Dev

Appinion Dev is a software-focused organization creating digital products and business platforms that help teams improve efficiency, productivity, and service delivery.

From internal management tools to customer-facing applications, we design and develop systems that are built for real-world use, long-term growth, and smooth operations.

---

## What We Build

### Healthcare & Care Delivery
We develop digital systems and platforms to support care operations, telehealth, patient workflows, and health-focused experiences.

### Business & Enterprise Systems
Our solutions include HR systems, administration portals, learning management tools, requisition management, and operational tracking platforms.

### Ecommerce & Commerce
We build storefronts, admin dashboards, and digital commerce experiences for modern retail and online business operations.

### Mobile & User Engagement
We create mobile experiences, customer apps, and service-driven interfaces that support daily operations and customer interaction.
